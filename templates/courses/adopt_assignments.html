Adopting an assignment (or an entire assignment group) makes it available in {{ course.name }} without copying it: the original course still owns the assignment, and any edits made there will show up here too. Behind the scenes, adopting just creates your own instructor submission for the assignment in this course, which is what makes it appear in the course's assignment lists, the grading views, the Feedback Editor, and so on. If you want your own editable copy instead, use Make Forks.
